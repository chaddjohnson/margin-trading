## Findings

- **Question 1 — did shallow books earn money in B5's genuinely idle time?** Barely, and only in the frozen-B5 phases.
  First-order idle-clean shallow net over 2020–2023 (lots whose reservation-to-exit interval never overlaps a B5 slot
  interval): 4B +$1,906, 4C +$7,826; negative in every 4D account (4D-A −$30,560, 4D-B −$26,166, 4D-C −$16,688). The
  idle-then-conflicting lots — started in idle time, then still open when B5 wanted the slot — lost more (4B −$10,725,
  4C −$8,729), so the shallow tier's total was negative everywhere. All figures are after 11% interest and 5 bps per side.
- **Question 2 — were B5's best deep-dip trades preserved?** For the frozen-B5 procedures, mostly: 4B, 4C, 4D-A and
  4D-A0P missed 5–10 of B5's 80 lots in 2020–2023 and none of B5's ten largest winners. The trades they gave up were
  mid-sized KRE, XBI and VGT trades in 2021–2022 (e.g. 4B and 4D-A0P missed KRE 2021-12-21, +$11,799, and KRE
  2021-09-22, +$8,507; 4D-A missed VGT 2022-07-05, +$10,956, and XBI 2022-05-12, +$10,542). 4C delayed eight B5 trades
  into mirror lots (P&L-weighted preservation 87.7%: delays cost money even when the trade was kept). The reoptimized
  deep tiers broke the incumbent: 4D-B missed 35 lots including five of the top ten (GDX's COVID trades of 2020-03-16,
  +$37,416, 2020-03-23, +$16,091, and 2020-04-01, +$27,571; GDX 2022-11-03, +$22,376; VGT 2020-10-28, +$13,031); 4D-C0
  missed 24 (GDX 2022-11-03 and XRT 2023-01-03 among them); 4D-B0 and 4D-C each missed XRT 2023-01-03 (+$13,140). Every
  lot's status is in the table above and in `results/tables/b5_trade_preservation.csv`.
