## Findings

- **4D was run in full, independently of 4A–4C** (whose evaluation years were not computed until every fold was
  sealed). All 24 window × experiment searches spent at least their frozen floor (table above); the predeclared
  training-only extension (+50% generations) triggered in 9 of them. With H, 4D-A, 4D-B and 4D-C are complete.
- **The optimizer found large in-sample gains.** Every filled fold pick had a positive development Δnet (4D-A +$107,231
  to +$125,481; 4D-B +$132,224 to +$208,038; 4D-C +$166,816 to +$184,090), robust to its own ±10% development
  perturbations by the §8 rule. F20's 4D-A, 4D-A0P, 4D-C, 4D-B0 and 4D-C0 picks were no-fill: no candidate passed the
  robust step (B5 funded only two lots in 2017–2019, so F20's drawdown cap was min($35,000, B5's $10,440 + $5,000) =
  $15,440; 4D-B0 had no feasible configuration at all).
- **None of it carried into the held-back years.** Of the 26 filled fold picks of all procedures, only four gained in
  their evaluation year (4B 2023 +$524, 4C 2020 +$6,385, 4C 2023 +$524, 4D-B 2021 +$29,908); four were exactly flat and
  18 lost. Summed over the filled picks, development Δ was +$2,661,008 and evaluation-year Δ −$534,665. Chained carry
  accounts: **4D-A −$67,337, 4D-B −$178,741, 4D-C −$101,768**; controls 4D-A0P −$87,312, 4D-B0 −$13,140, 4D-C0
  −$123,403. The selection skill is negative or weak: Spearman(development Δ, evaluation Δ) over the 100 best
  development configurations is −0.38 (4D-B), +0.56 (4D-A), +0.29 (4D-C); every experiment's ΣΔ is below zero and none
  clears the frozen E11 null test (PHASE_4E).
- **Where the losses came from (question 4).** *Deep-tier revisions* were the most damaging: 4D-B's reoptimized deep
  tier missed 35 of B5's 80 lots in 2020–2023, including five of B5's ten largest winners ($200,012 of profitable B5 net
  missed; P&L-weighted retention 51.2%), and its minute drawdown reached $47,510. The *shallow tier* lost money in every
  4D account (shallow-funded net −$33,529 / −$44,418 / −$49,411 for 4D-A/B/C) and made 4D-B and 4D-C worse than their
  own shallow-off ablations (by −$86,557 and −$64,304). The *allocator* mattered only in relative terms: 4D-A beat its
  same-budget P0 control 4D-A0P by +$19,975 and 4D-C beat 4D-C0 by +$21,635, but all four lost to B5; 4D-B lost to
  4D-B0 by −$165,601 (E6 fails). The pattern — large, perturbation-robust development gains that reverse out of sample,
  with low agreement between the fold picks' book sets (Jaccard 0.08–0.38 for 4D-A/B/C) — is the signature of **overfitting to
  development-window idiosyncrasies**, not of a stable complementary edge.
- **H (all 2016–2023 data, in-sample description only).** H's 4D-B selection is no-fill; its 4D-C pick (a broad EMB
  deep book added to B5, four shallow books, P2 with gate G1) shows an in-sample Δnet of +$165,158 — the same kind of
  development gain that every fold pick showed before reversing in its evaluation year. It is not evidence and, with no
  experiment labelled "historically promising", it is not a nominee.
- **Registered bias.** 4D-B neighbourhoods and 4D-C incumbent options are centred on B5's hindsight-chosen vectors, which
  biases fold Δ against moving away from them; the clean contrast 4D-B versus 4D-B0 (same inheritance) is also negative.
