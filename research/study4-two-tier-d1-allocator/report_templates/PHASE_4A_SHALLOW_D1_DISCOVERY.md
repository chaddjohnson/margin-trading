# Phase 4A — shallow-dip D1 discovery

Generated by `scripts/report.py` from the sealed records `results/<window>/4A/RECORD.json` and `SPEC/RECORD.json`

each window only; every figure is in-sample for its window.

## What was run

For every window (F20, F21, F22, F23, H) and every U61 symbol whose shallow band is non-empty: 4,096 scrambled-Sobol D1
vectors in the band plus 1,024 local refinements (16 diverse seeds × 64), each traced standalone on the window's
development sessions together with its frequency-reference vector (X = 0.85σ). Bands are ETF-relative: X ∈ [max(3.0%,
0.15σ), min(0.45σ, 15.0%)], and on a B5 symbol X ≤ 0.6 × the B5 book's X; a vector also needs at least max(2 per year,
1.5 × its reference vector's) development decisions to count as shallow. The library keeps, per symbol and X tertile,
up to four distinct members (L1 best standalone net, L2 best B5-idle-clean net, L3 best idle-clean net per held day,
L4 best net minus minute DD), at least 0.15 apart, up to 12 per symbol. Standalone profit is not a hard screen.

## Findings

- **Coverage — the floor was not fully spent (disclosure D1).** In every window every symbol with a non-empty shallow
  band received its 4,096 Sobol vectors, but the refinement never topped up when fewer than 16 seeds passed its filters
  (8 best by net within $35,000 minute DD, 8 best by idle-clean net, greedily diverse), so 48 symbol-windows fell short
  of the frozen floor of 5,120: **1,410,048 of 1,428,480 planned vectors (98.71%)**, F20 exact, F21–F23 and H short
  (table below). The study therefore reports **4A as incomplete (98.71% of its floor)**; nothing was rerun after the
  outcomes. The symbols without a band are the low-volatility bond ETFs listed above.

<!-- TABLE 4A_coverage -->

- **Shallow D1 looks profitable in-sample, by construction.** Most library members have a positive standalone
  development net, and a majority have a positive *B5-idle-clean* development net (the net of their lots that never
  overlap a B5 slot interval) — but the library keeps the best vectors of 5,120 per symbol on exactly these figures, so
  these are selected in-sample maxima, not evidence. The held-back years (Phase 4E) show they did not carry forward: in
  every chained account the funded shallow lots lost money in total (4B −$8,820, 4C −$903, 4D-A −$33,529; table in
  `PHASE_4E…`), and their first-order idle-clean net was small (4B +$1,906, 4C +$7,826) or negative (every 4D account).
- **The roles are as declared.** Every library member satisfies the frozen shallow role (X within
  [max(3.0%, 0.15σ), min(0.45σ, 15%)], ≤ 0.6 × the B5 book's X on B5 symbols) and the frequency requirement; standalone
  profit was not a hard screen, so loss-making shallow vectors could enter later phases if they fitted B5's idle time.
- 2022 and 2023 libraries (F23, H) are in-sample for years the designer knew; they describe, they do not validate.
