## Findings

- **Completeness reading (disclosure D1).** The labels in this report are the frozen rubric code's (V2 pass). 4A's
  vector floor was not fully spent (98.71%; `PHASE_4A…`); under the protocol's literal V2 every label would be
  "invalid/incomplete". The economics below are unaffected.
- **Order of operations.** Every fold selection was sealed (last 2026-10-30T02:43:47Z) and the evaluation code frozen
  before the evaluation door opened. The first evaluation run crashed in the 4D-B perturbation step before sealing
  anything (amendment A5: the perturbation context lacked B5's books); it had printed B5's yearly increments and the
  chained/reset ΣΔ of 4B, 4C, 4D-A and 4D-A0P. The fix was mechanical; the evaluation was refrozen and rerun in full and
  reproduced those printed figures exactly. H ran after the evaluation was sealed.
- **The verdict object fails for every procedure.** B5's continuous run earned +$352,588 over 2020–2023. Every chained
  carry account is below it: 4B −$27,669, 4C −$48,533, 4D-A −$67,337, 4D-A0P −$87,312, 4D-B −$178,741, 4D-B0 −$13,140,
  4D-C −$101,768, 4D-C0 −$123,403. E1 (ΣΔ ≥ +$20,000 with ≥ 3 positive years) fails for all; the reset accounting is
  negative for all. Five accounts also exceed the fixed-slot risk cap (E3; minute DD $33,493 to $55,226 against
  $31,510), so their frozen label is risk-incompatible; the other three are "no demonstrated incremental benefit".
- **Robustness (question 5): there is no incremental profit to be robust.** ΣΔ is negative in every cell of the
  8/11/14% × 0/5/10 bps grid, under the 300 s replacement latency (the protocol's "+5 minutes" was implemented as an
  absolute 300 s, +4 minutes over the 60 s base), 25 bps stop slippage, next-open stop fills and all three missing-bar
  seeds (E7, E7b fail everywhere). Evaluation-year perturbations of the fold picks are positive in
  only 0–30% of cases, with medians ≤ $0 (E5 fails). The losses are concentrated in the 2022 bear market (the worst
  year for 4C, 4D-A, 4D-A0P, 4D-B, 4D-C and 4D-C0); 4B's worst year was 2021 (−$17,409), when B5 itself earned only
  $15,702.
- **The idle time was there, the edge was not.** B5's longest idle stretches of 2020–2023 were in 2021 (76, 58 and 41
  days; table above). Every two-tier procedure funded shallow lots in them; only 4B and 4C made money in the longest
  gap (+$8,354, +$8,970) and the 4D accounts lost in most gaps — the shallow books' entries in quiet uptrends did not
  pay for their losses elsewhere.
- **No selection skill.** The sealed picks did no better than random champions: for seven of the eight procedures ΣΔ is
  below the 75th percentile of the random-champion null (4D-A0P is above it at −$87,312 versus −$91,560, but its
  Spearman correlation is negative), and Spearman(development Δ, evaluation Δ) over the 100 best development
  configurations is negative for 4B, 4C, 4D-A0P, 4D-B and 4D-B0. E11 fails everywhere.
- **Criteria that pass vacuously.** E10 passes for 4B and 4C only because ΣΔ is negative (a positive idle-clean net is
  then ≥ 50% of ΣΔ); E6 passes for the deep-only controls 4D-B0 and 4D-C0 because its two-tier and own-control clauses
  do not apply to them. Neither changes a label (E1 fails first). E11's Spearman uses
  the 100 best development configurations with ordinal ranks (no tie-averaging; ties are heavy); with tie-averaged ranks
  4D-B0's is −0.95 instead of −0.82, and pooling the best and random rows makes 4D-B's +0.01 — E11 still fails for every
  procedure because ΣΔ is below the null's 75th percentile (final review, finding 6). E4 is not evaluated, as declared before results: the
  frozen portfolio engine cannot express a Study 4 pool (B5's own overlay reproduced its anchor, $2,270,264.33, exactly).
- **4E status: complete** (fold evaluations, chained carry and reset accounts, perturbations, one-at-a-time profiles,
  costs, stresses, seed stability, skill, null, regime diagnostics and B5's idle gaps; reference checks fast =
  reference 16/16). Not computed: the §10.7 count of idle minutes after a deep lot exits while a preempted shallow
  virtual trade is still open (the records keep switch and lot timing, not virtual-trade exits of preempted books),
  and 2018 Q4, a development-period event.
