## 0. The exact scientific question and non-negotiable intent

Can we **meaningfully increase the net profitability of the existing B5 shared-margin portfolio during periods when its capital would otherwise be idle** by introducing lower-priority D1 phenotypes with shallower, more frequent dip entries, while **preserving nearly all of B5's valuable high-priority, deep-dip trades**, and without unacceptable drawdown, carrying costs or preemption losses?

**This is primarily a D1 research program, NOT another momentum study.** Start with the **established D1 entry, exit, continuation, stop, losing-hold, buffered-loss and reentry architecture** as actually implemented and used by the repository. Optimize numerical phenotypes of that architecture, including shallower initial-dip thresholds and associated interacting parameters, without silently introducing a new momentum-entry system. Alternative trend-continuation or momentum mechanisms are **secondary, low priority and outside the binding Study 4 matrix unless a distinct, predeclared small exploratory arm is justified**. Do not revive or promote any failed Study 3 momentum candidate as an assumed solution.

**High-priority tier:** initially, the **unchanged registered B5 books**, comprising VGT, XBI, XRT, KRE and GDX, with each one's exact registered phenotype and the existing allocator. The user's highest priorities include VGT and GDX, but do not assume the other three have identical dip sizes. Verify actual source vectors, rules, ordering and tie behavior.

**Low-priority tier:** D1 phenotypes designed for **more frequent entries on shallower dips**. They can trade the same symbol as a high-priority book if modeled as distinct, separately managed logical strategies. They consume the **same single shared funded margin slot**, not additional simultaneous borrowed allocations. A credible actionable high-priority signal can take precedence over a low-priority position according to a separately tested, causal preemption policy.

**Immutable control:** the original B5 phenotype set, allocator rules, funding semantics, historical records, and prospective shadow protocol must remain unchanged. Frozen B5 remains the primary reference in EVERY phase, including 4D. A jointly optimized deep-tier configuration is a **new challenger**, never an in-place update to B5.
