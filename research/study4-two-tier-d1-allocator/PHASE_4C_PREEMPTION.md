## What was run (per window, development data only)

Fifteen policies — P0, P0M (late funding), P1 (unconditional), P2 (net breakeven), P2L (legacy mechanics), P3 with loss
budgets $500/$1,000/$2,000/$3,500 × {any deep decision, deep-path decisions only}, P4 with deadlines of 0 and 1 sessions —
on the 4B pick's set (with its gate), the SA set, the ten best 4B singles and the five best 4B subsets. Switches follow
the frozen contract: sale at the shallow symbol's first trade at or after the deep decision, mirror purchase only after
the sale is known (one minute later at least), fill tests as immediate-or-cancel limits, supersession and staleness
rules as amended (A1–A3).

  procedure used P2 (only H's in-sample 4D-C pick does), so the legacy-replacement stress (for P2 fold picks) does not
  apply.
- **Held-back years:** fold Δ +$6,385 (2020), −$15,303 (2021), −$26,039 (2022), +$524 (2023); chained ΣΔ **−$48,533**
  (reset −$50,971). E1 fails; E3 passes (minute DD $26,510 = B5's); E2 fails (P&L-weighted preservation 87.7% < 90%).
  Frozen label: **no demonstrated incremental benefit**.
- **Preemption versus none, when the shallow lot is losing** (ablations of the chained 4C account, biased in favour of
  the pick): preemption off (P0, same books) −$88,903; late funding (P0M) −$42,180; the pick −$48,533. Preemption cut
  the damage of the idle-only design roughly in half, but every variant lost to B5. Forced sales in the 2022 bear
  market (P4(1): XBI deep trades selling GDX shallow lots) cost up to −$5,717 per switch; the per-switch ledger is in
  `results/ledgers/4C_chained_switches.csv`. The same ordering holds for 4D-A (pick −$67,337; P0M −$102,605; P0
  −$137,631): conditional or unconditional preemption beats non-preemption only in the relative sense.
