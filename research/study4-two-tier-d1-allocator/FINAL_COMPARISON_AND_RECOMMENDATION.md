## Findings

### The verdict in one paragraph

Under the frozen rubric **no Study 4 procedure beat B5**. Every chained 2020–2023 account — the configurations each
fold selected on its own preceding years, traded in the following year — finished below B5's continuous run (4B
−$27,669, 4C −$48,533, 4D-A −$67,337, 4D-B −$178,741, 4D-C −$101,768; controls 4D-A0P −$87,312, 4D-B0 −$13,140, 4D-C0
−$123,403, against B5's +$352,588). Three procedures are labelled **no demonstrated incremental benefit** (4C, 4D-A,
4D-B0) and five **risk-incompatible** (4B, 4D-A0P, 4D-B, 4D-C, 4D-C0: chained minute drawdown above min($35,000,
B5's + $5,000) = $31,510). These are the labels of the rubric **as frozen in code**, under which validity
(V1) and completeness (V2) pass. **Under the literal protocol text of V2** ("every frozen budget spent") V2 fails,
because 4A's refinement left 18,432 of 1,428,480 planned 4A vectors (1.29%) unevaluated in F21–F23 and H (disclosure
D1, found by the final review), and **every label would be "invalid/incomplete"**; which reading binds is the user's
decision. Either way the negative result does not rest on an unspent or underpowered search: every 4D floor was spent in
every window, the
fast engine equals an independent reference implementation, B5 is reproduced to the cent, and the negative sign holds
across all costs, stresses, perturbations and both account conventions. **There is no nominee** under either reading,
and none could have been nominated without a portfolio overlay for E4 (declared before results).

### The brief's six questions

1. **Shallow books in B5's idle time.** Only marginally positive, and only with B5 frozen: the first-order idle-clean
   shallow net over 2020–2023 was +$1,906 (4B) and +$7,826 (4C) after 11% interest and costs, and negative in every 4D
   account. Lots that started in idle time but were still open when B5 wanted the slot lost more, so the shallow tier's
   total was negative in every two-tier account (DEEP_TIER report).
2. **B5's deep-dip trades.** With B5's books frozen, B5's ten largest 2020–2023 winners were all kept; 5–10 of its 80
   lots were missed, mostly mid-sized KRE, XBI and VGT trades of 2021–2022, and 4C delayed eight into mirror lots. The
   reoptimized deep tier of 4D-B missed 35 lots, including GDX's three COVID trades and two more of B5's ten largest winners. The
   per-trade table lists every B5 lot as exact, delayed, altered or missed for every procedure.
3. **Preemption.** Preemption reduced the damage relative to idle-only funding but never produced a gain: 4C's pick
   −$48,533 versus −$88,903 with preemption off and −$42,180 with late funding; 4D-A −$67,337 versus −$137,631 (P0) and
   −$102,605 (P0M); the same-budget P0 control 4D-A0P −$87,312. Net-breakeven (P2) and legacy (P2L) policies were
   never selected in any fold (H's in-sample 4D-C pick uses P2); of the 14 fold picks of 4C and 4D-A/B/C, unconditional P1 was chosen 7 times, deadline P4
   3 times, loss-budget P3 twice (4D-B F20 $1,250; 4D-A F22 $1,000) and late funding P0M twice. Forced sales of losing
   shallow lots cost up to −$9,603 per switch.
4. **Joint optimization.** It found large development gains (+$107k to +$208k per fold pick) that reversed in every
   held-back year but one (4D-B 2021). None of 4D-A, 4D-B, 4D-C won. The losses came from the deep-tier revisions (4D-B
   versus its deep-only control −$165,601; 35 missed B5 lots), from the shallow tier (worse than the shallow-off
   ablation in 4D-B and 4D-C by −$86,557 and −$64,304) and not from the allocator (4D-A beat 4D-A0P by +$19,975 and 4D-C
   beat 4D-C0 by +$21,635, both still negative against B5). The development-versus-evaluation reversal (26 filled fold
   picks: Σ development Δ +$2.66 million, Σ evaluation-year Δ −$534,665) is the signature of overfitting.
5. **Robustness.** There was no incremental profit to be robust: ΣΔ is negative at every interest/cost setting, under
   every execution stress (300 s replacement latency, 25 bps stop slippage, next-open stop fills) and missing-bar seed, and in the reset accounting; evaluation-year perturbations were positive
   in 0–30% of cases; the sealed picks did worse than the 75th percentile of random champions in seven of eight
   procedures. The few positive fold-years (4C 2020 +$6,385; 4D-B 2021 +$29,908; 4B and 4C 2023 +$524 each) were isolated.
6. **What would be required before any later gate.** No Study 4 configuration merits the separately authorised
   2024–2025 validation, the 2026 holdout or prospective shadow registration. Re-entering this line of work would need
   a **new question with its own protocol** (a new study namespace), for example a shallow role that is gated on B5's
   expected idle time or on a regime signal fixed in advance, judged by the same chronological fold design and rubric,
   plus an implemented portfolio overlay so that E4 can be evaluated. Only a procedure labelled "historically promising
   but unvalidated" under such a protocol, with its H pick meeting E3, would be a candidate for a 2024–2025 request — and
   that request, the 2026 holdout and any shadow registration each need the user's separate authorisation.

### Strongest historically interesting configurations (none validated)

No procedure reached "historically promising but unvalidated". The closest to B5 were the deep-only control 4D-B0
(−$13,140: one missed XRT trade in 2023) and the frozen-B5 integrations 4B and 4C, which kept B5's large trades and
earned a small idle-clean profit but lost more on conflicting lots. The single positive fold-year of note, 4D-B's 2021
pick (+$29,908), sits inside a −$178,741 chained account. The H picks above (chosen on all of 2016–2023) are in-sample descriptions only: the largest, 4D-C's
(+$165,158 in-sample), has the same profile as the fold picks whose development gains reversed; H's 4D-B is no-fill.

### Recommendation and next gate

Keep **B5 unchanged** as the fixed-slot control; do not adopt any Study 4 shallow tier, preemption policy or deep-tier
revision. Leave 2024–2025 and 2026 unopened for Study 4. The next gate is the user's review of this branch; any further
study (a new namespace and protocol), any 2024+ data access and any change to live, paper or shadow configurations
requires the user's explicit instruction.

### Disclosures that bear on the verdict

- In-sample nature: every fold year had been seen by earlier studies; the designer knew the 2020 crash, the 2022 bear
  market and B5's 2022–2023 outcomes; B5 itself was chosen with 2016–2026 hindsight. These biases favour B5-derived
  challengers (4D-B, 4D-C) and B5 alike; they cannot turn the negative result positive.
- F20's development window gave B5 only two funded lots (2017–2019), so F20 picks were selected against a nearly idle
  incumbent.
- Amendment A5: the first evaluation run crashed and printed four procedures' chained results before the fix; the rerun
  reproduced them exactly (AMENDMENTS.md).
- The frozen `ShadowBook` manages a mirror lot by the virtual position's exit state (ENGINE_CONTRACT §5.11).
