## Findings

- **Development (in-sample): every fold produced a pick**, with development Δnet of +$33,674 to +$80,523 against B5 and
  perturbation medians of +$19,699 to +$42,648 (71–96% of perturbations positive). On F20's development window B5
  itself funded only two lots in 2017–2019, so almost any profitable shallow book looked additive there.
- **Held-back years: the gains did not survive.** Fold Δ by evaluation year: −$6,983 (2020), −$17,409 (2021), −$0
  (2022, the pick funded no shallow lot), +$524 (2023). The chained carry account loses **−$27,669** against B5 over
  2020–2023 (reset accounting −$55,199); E1 fails. Its minute drawdown, **$33,493 (33.5% of the slot)**, exceeds the
  frozen cap min($35,000, B5's $26,510 + $5,000) = $31,510, so the frozen label is **risk-incompatible**.
- **Mechanism: idle-only is not harmless.** Under P0 a funded shallow lot runs to its own exit; a B5 signal that arrives
  meanwhile is refused. In 2021 the F21 pick's shallow lots (and the B5 lots whose timing they shifted) held the slot when
  B5's profitable trades came (fold P&L-weighted preservation 27.2%); over the chained years 7 of B5's 80 lots were missed (profitable B5 net $22,601 given up; none of
  B5's ten largest winners). The shallow lots themselves netted −$8,820: +$1,906 idle-clean, −$10,725 from lots that
  started in idle time and then overlapped B5's slot intervals.
- **4B status: complete** (all four folds sealed and evaluated; H below).
