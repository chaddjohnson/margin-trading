# Phase 3A: early entry stratified by correction depth (descriptive, both stop models)

**Question.** Does early entry help in shallow pullbacks and hurt in deep corrections, or the reverse? The protocol froze four depth bands before any result existed, and every decision is classified into a band at the moment it was made.

**Short answer:**

- **Early entry loses in every band** once the 2020 crash is set aside, so its merits do not reverse with depth.
- **The losses are largest in SHALLOW pullbacks.** That is where most decisions happen, and where the close-time rule is cheapest.
- **The EXTREME band shows both effects** the brief asks about:
  - one large beneficial rebound capture: the February–April 2020 crash, +$1.5k to +$35k depending on policy;
  - harmful premature entries in every other extreme episode (2018, 2022, 2025), with **no positive EXTREME episode outside 2020 for 8 of the 10 unconditional policies** under the daily stop.
- **Samples in the deep bands are small** when counted as independent market episodes rather than trades.

## Band definitions and decision-time rule

| Band | Depth `d = 1 − completedMinuteClose / recentHigh` |
|---|---|
| SHALLOW | 2.5% ≤ d < 5% |
| MODERATE | 5% ≤ d < 10% |
| DEEP | 10% ≤ d < 15% |
| EXTREME | d ≥ 15% |

How a decision is classified:

- **Reference high.** `recentHigh` is the frozen high of the previous 78 completed sessions: the strategy's own rolling reference, not an all-time high. It is fixed for the whole session.
- **Price.** The band comes from the close of the completed minute that triggered the decision. For `CLOSE_1M`, that is the incumbent's final decision minute.
- **No look-ahead.** No session high, low or close, trade outcome, or later reference high enters the classification. A session's band can change minute by minute; each record carries the band at its own decision instant.

The no-look-ahead property is tested in three places:

- `src/intraday-study/tests/policies.test.ts`, "records the band at the decision instant";
- the same file's causality property test, where perturbing every later minute never changes a decision;
- the independent replay ([replay/REPLAY_REPORT.md](replay/REPLAY_REPORT.md)), which reproduces the bands and trades exactly.

The per-session record of decision-time facts is [results/session_facts.csv](results/session_facts.csv). It holds the final-decision band, the first qualifying minute from 09:35 and from 10:00 with their bands, and the deepest minute. Per-trade bands are in every `results/runs/*/*/trades.csv` (`band`, `decision_depth`, `decision_time_et`).

**A structural caveat.** For the `FIRST_*` rules, the decision is the *first* minute at 2.5% below the reference. It is therefore SHALLOW by construction unless the session opened with a gap down through 5%. Their band mix is mechanically skewed toward SHALLOW. The confirmation rules (`PERSIST`, `STABLE`, `REBOUND`) and `CLOSE_1M` decide later and spread across the bands more naturally.

- **Setups:** sessions that start flat and whose first qualifying eligible minute, or `CLOSE_1M`'s qualifying final decision, falls in the band.

## 5. Findings (diagnostic, not claims of superiority)

**Do early entry's relative merits change materially with correction size?** Only in degree, not in sign, once the single 2020 crash is set aside.

- **SHALLOW (2.5–5%).** This is where most of the damage is: −$34k to −$71k of full-path Δ per unconditional policy under the daily stop. Only 8–16 of the 27–37 SHALLOW episodes per policy are positive.
  - This is also where the 57 dip-and-recovery opportunities live. They are real, but each one occupies the single position slot, and the many shallow first touches that keep falling (premature entries) cost more.
- **MODERATE (5–10%).** Mixed to negative for the unconditional rules: +$0.2k to −$46k. For the conditional policies, which buy only MODERATE (and DEEP/EXTREME) dips early, it is negative in all six policy × stop cases, between −$7.6k and −$33.9k.
- **DEEP (10–15%).** Small and mostly negative: −$17k to +$5k. There are only 2–8 episodes per policy.
- **EXTREME (≥ 15%).** The only band with sizeable early-entry gains, and they all come from **one** episode: February–April 2020. That episode is worth +$1.5k to +$28.5k per policy under the daily stop and up to +$35.1k under the minute stop.
  - **2018, 2022 and 2025.** The other EXTREME episodes, from the late-2018 selloff, the 2022 bear market and the April 2025 drawdown, are negative: 2–4 episodes per policy, −$4k to −$44k.
  - **Positive EXTREME episodes outside 2020.** Under the daily stop, only `FIRST_1200` and `FIRST_1400` have even one positive EXTREME episode outside 2020. `PERSIST_30` and `STABLE_30` have none at all outside 2020.

**Do larger declines show more harmful early entries, more beneficial rebound captures, or both?** Both, but each at the scale of single episodes.

- **The beneficial side is one V-shaped crash.** In March 2020, buying at 10:00 on days that later recovered beat the 15:59 entry.
- **The harmful side is the grinding declines.** Examples:
  - `FIRST_1000` on 2022-04-22 bought at 381.73, against a close-rule fill of 372.67;
  - it bought again on 2022-06-06 at 365.64, against 360.21, and was sold by the maximum hold at 324.71.

  Section 3 shows these side by side.
- **The close rule's own deep entries are its best trades.** `CLOSE_1M`'s 25 EXTREME entries won 23 times, for $51,962 and a mean of $2,078. Earlier entry in that band gives up price: five of the six confirmation rules pay 0.37%–0.85% *more* than the same session's close-rule fill. `REBOUND_03` is the exception, paying 0.04% less.

**Are there adequate observations per band?**

- **By trades, yes.** Under the daily stop, every band has ≥ 23 entries for every policy, and no band is "no sample" or "insufficient sample".
- **By independent market episodes, no.** The incumbent's 25 EXTREME entries fall into 2018 (2), 2020 (3), 2022 (17) and 2025 (3). The early-entry EXTREME divergences reduce to 1–5 episodes per policy.

Any statement about the EXTREME band is therefore a statement about roughly four market events, one of which (2020) dominates.

**After excluding the single largest contributing episode**, which is the 2020 crash for most policies, no band shows a positive early-entry effect for any unconditional policy. This exclusion is a diagnostic only and was not used to select or modify anything.
