# REPORT — Priority 3C: a fixed-anchor GDX Growth partner search beside C4-refined, C4 and VGT Growth #4

Research only, not deployed. Growth·GDX was searched **alone** while every other book's parameters stayed frozen, in
**one shared $100,000 slot** under the current funding lifecycle **L0** (a refused entry keeps its virtual trade;
funding never feeds back; freed capital never revives a refusal), one exclusive funded slot, no compounding or
preemption, same-instant order D1 first then GDX before VGT, 5 bps per side, $0 commission, 11% simple interest
(365-day basis). **DD** is the maximum **minute** drawdown of the marked net; DD % = DD ÷ the fixed $100,000. The
ceiling is **$35,000**; historical compliance with it is not a guarantee of future drawdown.

- **Three separate objectives, reported in the brief's order:**
  - **R (lead): C4-refined** — Priority 3B's `C24/informed-fixed#1 profit` (VG #2's two D1 books unchanged + a refined
    VGT Growth book), an alias here, not a new phenotype;
  - **O: original C4** — VG #2 Balanced (`S8-VG-F24-3`) + VGT Growth #4 (`420cd989e4fa7749`);
  - **G (companion): VGT Growth #4 alone**, no D1 — a two-book Growth pool.
  Each arm's **baseline** is its anchors with GDX Growth OFF; it is a real member and the no-improvement fallback. The
  arms had unequal search budgets by design (R 66.7%); this is a priority choice, not a method comparison.
- **Development and every selection:** 2016-01-04 → 2024-12-31. **Validation:** 2025-01-02 → 2026-10-30 (the verified
  common complete endpoint), opened once after the frozen selection was committed. It is **validation on previously
  examined history, not an untouched holdout**: every anchor and control was chosen or studied with 2025–2026 in view.
- **Incumbent-informed research:** the anchors were chosen with later data in view, so even the broadly initialized GDX
  lanes are anchor-conditional (departure D1); the earlier-cutoff repeats are **descriptive**, never chronological
  evidence of an independent procedure.
- Every figure comes from sealed records (`results/`); complete tables are in `results/tables/`. No charts (standard
  2.0.0 §7.7). C4 and C4-refined stay distinct, immutable baselines; no user preference between them is recorded here.

## 1. Conclusion

**Keeping GDX Growth OFF remains preferable for all three baselines** under the frozen recommendation rule, and the
economic evidence agrees. Searching GDX alone did raise **development** profit beside every anchor — by about
$39,000 beside C4-refined and C4, and by about $120,000 beside VGT #4 alone — but:

- **No partner dominated C4-refined or C4** on the four frozen development measures. Several came very close: equal
  centre DD, higher confirmation p10, and a p90 DD only $47–$298 worse, inside finite-draw uncertainty. Read:
  development parity or slightly better, not a demonstrated improvement.

  2019 gold rally (+$31,353.89, 80% of the increment). The same 2019 trade recurs across the R and O partners.
- **In 2025–2026 the selected GDX books barely traded.** The R and G primaries opened **no** GDX lot after 2024, so their

  whole-run DD still rose from $19,477.66 to $27,251.67, measured from a higher October 2024 peak (inside its last GDX

### The brief's questions

1. **Did searching GDX alone improve fixed C4-refined?** **On development, in profit only; not as a demonstrated
   improvement.**
   - All 12 searched partners qualified (Q1–Q7). All earn more at the centre (+$21,324.34 to +$38,990.80). **None
     dominates** C4-refined, so there is no partner candidate.
   - The closest misses:
     - `C24/R/seeded#2 robust`: +$24,486.31, the same centre DD, p10 +$14,830.16, p90 DD only **+$47.43** worse;
     - `C24/R/broad-rise#3 balanced`: +$37,489.92, p10 +$16,505.59, p90 +$115.67, centre DD +$1,416.58.
     Both p90 differences lie inside the finite-draw uncertainty of the two 95% order-statistic intervals.
   - In validation the role holders matched C4-refined or exceeded it slightly: the primary by $0.00, the robust and
     balanced holders by +$1,766.52, each with one GDX lot.
   - The economic class is a **risk/profit trade-off**: a development profit gain with a tail that is equal within noise
     or slightly heavier. Its evidence is narrow: it is concentrated in one 2019 GDX trade and adds nothing in 2025–2026.
2. **Did it improve original C4?** **The same picture.**
   - All 9 partners qualified (+$4,295.00 to +$38,799.70); none dominates.
   - The closest misses:
     - `C24/O/seeded#2 robust`: +$16,106.71, centre DD −$51.35, p10 +$14,289.76, p90 **+$88.13**;
     - `C24/O/broad-rise#1 profit`: +$36,843.11, centre DD −$51.35, p10 +$8,314.78, p90 +$297.91.
   - The primary earned +$144.73 in validation.
   - **A (C4 + Appendix A) failed Q3 again** ($36,038.34) **and Q7** (GDX-only p90 $36,256.71), as sealed before.
3. **Did it improve fixed VGT #4 without D1?** **On development, by a lot. In 2025–2026, not at all.**
   - VGT #4 alone leaves the slot idle in most sessions (836 held of 2,264), so a GDX book has room. The primary adds 11
     GDX lots and +$122,859.06; the robust holder adds +$118,031.76 with a p90 DD only +$1,423.51 above #4's (within
     noise).
   - None of these books traded GDX after 2024: their validation P&L equals #4 alone's ($52,892.75).
   - The only partner dominating #4 alone (the frozen partner candidate, `C24/G/broad-breakout#3 balanced`) is trivial:
     one GDX lot in 2016, +$863.66. In validation it traded 6 GDX lots and **lost $7,913.64 against the baseline**,
     with a reset DD of $18,690.16 against $9,916.01.
   - GP4 (#4 + Appendix A) **fails Q3, Q4 and Q7**.
4. **Did the same GDX phenotype transfer between objectives?** **Partly, one way only** (complete pooled reruns,
   unchanged books, report-only; §6).
   - The **R** books also added profit beside C4 (+$8,379.65 to +$48,846.62) and beside #4 alone (+$8,379.65 to
     +$45,893.22).
   - The **G** books, built to fill #4's idle slot, **lost** money beside C4-refined (−$1,118.82 to −$13,849.24) and
     mostly beside C4 (−$8,979.75 to −$14,244.94; the one exception, G seeded#2, +$5,948.47). The G primary's book
     fails Q3 and Q7 beside both.
   - The **O** books were mixed.
   - A GDX partner is a function of how its anchors occupy the slot; a winner for #4 alone is not a winner for C4 or
     C4-refined.
5. **How much baseline opportunity did it displace?** **Little for the selected partners; a lot for Appendix A.**
   - The R primary displaced one VGT Growth lot (2019-06-04, −$1,977.68). The O primary displaced one (2023-11-02,
     −$14,514.22, followed by 68 idle closes) but newly admitted two D1·GDX lots in February 2024 (+$28,624.54).

     2020-06-25 (+$14,064.09), with 247 idle closes during those virtual holds.
6. **Is the result a stable addition, a risk/profit trade-off, or no improvement?**
   - **R and O:** risk/profit trade-offs on development (parity or better locally, never dominating), with no
     improvement after 2024.
   - **G:** a large development trade-off, plus a trivial "stable addition" that failed in validation.
   - **Does keeping GDX OFF remain preferable?** **Yes, for each baseline**: by the frozen rule, and on the evidence
     (episode concentration, no 2025–2026 contribution, negative descriptive fold repeats).

## 11. Limitations

- **Previously examined history and anchor provenance.** 2025–2026 had been examined before, and every anchor and
  control was chosen with it in view. The fold repeats cannot erase that provenance, and no claim of an independent
  procedure is made (D1).
- **Few GDX trades.** The R and O partners trade GDX 1–5 times in nine years; their increments are essentially one or
  two episodes. Neighbourhood stability on 2016–2024 measures robustness to nearby settings on that one history, not
  across periods; the fold repeats and the validation are the only out-of-sample-style evidence, and both are
  unfavourable or neutral.
- **Instrument sensitivity.** The near-dominance cases differ from the baselines by $47–$298 of p90 DD, inside
  finite-draw uncertainty; 3B's instrument orders one of them the other way. The labels are deterministic given the
  frozen instrument, not robust to resampling. That is why the frozen rule also requires validation support.
- **Unequal search budgets** (R 66.7%) by design; they say nothing about which method or baseline is better.
- **Not tested:** other funding lifecycles or allocation designs (Priority 5); standalone GDX Growth (4A); GDX-only D1 +
  Growth (4B); November–December 2026.
