# Dispositions — methodology review 1 (selection and statistics)

Written before any Stage 1c strategy ran on market data and before the protocol freeze. Each finding of
`METHODOLOGY_REVIEW_1.md` is listed with its disposition and where the change lands (PROTOCOL v1.1, ENGINE_CONTRACT,
EXPERIMENT_MATRIX v1.1, MECHANISM_CATALOG, code). "Adopted" means implemented as the reviewer proposed or in an
equivalent form stated here.

| 1 | MAJOR | **Adopted** | Common random numbers: every cell of a pair (entry, exit, contract) draws its Sobol points, local points and joint draws from **pair-level** labels over the pair's full dimension list (entry dims, exit dims, then four fixed rule slots a.N, b.C, c.X, c.Y); a cell uses its active slots only. Every masked first-pass and deep-global vector therefore has a rule-off twin (same index) in the pair's 000 cell. Per cell the paired Δnet / ΔminuteDD / ΔRoD distribution and the exact inert share (identical path hash with the twin) are reported (PROTOCOL §7.1, §7.2, §5). |
| 2 | MAJOR | **Adopted (ii), (i)** | Deep allocation is symmetric: the union over the 8 masks of the top-3 pairs by that mask's first-pass RoD champion score, plus a separate NET list (top-4 pairs by any-mask NET champion score). With CRN an inert masked cell reproduces its 000 twin exactly, so the "replica" effect is limited to rule vectors that act. The placebo (iii) is not needed under CRN and is not run. The residual asymmetry (P\* maximises over 8 masks, P\*-000 over one) is intrinsic to the question "does adding the rules to the search help forward" and is stated (PROTOCOL §7.3, §11). |
