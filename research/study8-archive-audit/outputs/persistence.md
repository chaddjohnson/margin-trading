# Validation persistence audit — sealed test results only

Generated by `python3 research/study8-archive-audit/scripts/persistence.py`. **Population: the 36 shortlist members of each fold (180), the only configurations with sealed test records.** Training figures are the fold's own confirmation evidence (3 × 128 neighbours on the fold's training door, `results/qualify/<T>/<F>/qualification.json`); test figures are the sealed test record (`results/test/<F>/test.json`: T status, test-window centre net, whole-run minute DD, test-family share and p90 on the run to the test end). No door was opened and no configuration was replayed. **All of 2016-01-04 → 2026-10-29, including every test window, had been inspected by the research programme before Study 8** (PROTOCOL.md §3.4); these are retrospective procedural statistics, not independent validation.

**Selection and survivorship.** Every member is a survivor of ~15,000 rung-1 evaluations in its track-fold and was shortlisted *because* its training neighbourhood was broad: training shares are compressed into 0.84–1.00, which attenuates any correlation (range restriction). Members of one track share books and often near-identical vectors, so the 36 per fold are not independent; folds overlap in time (F20's 2022 is F21's first test year) and every fold starts in 2016.
