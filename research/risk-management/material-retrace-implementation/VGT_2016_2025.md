**December 2018 (retracement reset).** The 2018-10-26 loss (−$1,903, price −1.45%) armed the
restriction with reference high 24.015. Under the frozen-high rule the reset needed 22.81 and the
previous 3% rule therefore waited for a 3% lower close, bought 2018-11-20 (−$87) and 2018-12-19 (−$2,903),
and then sat out the January–May 2019 recovery entirely. The retracement reset fired on 2018-11-07
(close ≥ 20.99 + 0.38 × (24.015 − 20.99) = 22.14), re-admitting the normal rules: the new strategy
bought 2018-11-12 (−$3,532), re-entered 3% lower on 2018-12-17 (−$3,631), was reset again on
2019-01-18 (episode low 18.71 → level 20.72) and bought 2019-01-22 @ 20.50, riding to 2019-05-13 for
**+$11,183**. Net effect of the episode: +$7,010 vs the previous rule, bought at the price of two
extra losing trades on the way down. (The material-loss-only variant did worse here, −$12,585, because
on its path the exemptions let it buy 2018-12-11 and absorb the Christmas-Eve crash, −$11,539; the
combined strategy's path never reached that entry.)

**2020 crash.** All restricted variants are identical: the −8.39% loss of 03-11 armed, the 3% level
was hit the next day (03-12 @ 23.71, −$2,432 after the gap-down stop), the next 3% level was hit at
the bottom (03-20 @ 22.43, +$7,420) and the profitable exit freed the 04-02 entry @ 24.67 for
**+$48,014** — the largest trade of the period. Neither new mechanism touched this sequence (both
losses were material; no reset fired before the 3% level).

**2022 bear market and January 2023 (material-loss exemption).** Through October the new strategy
tracks the previous 3% rule trade for trade (eight trades, −$3,865 at that point). The divergence is
the 2022-10-07 → 11-03 trade: −$1,306 net but only **−0.49% in price** (38.25 → 38.06), which the
previous rule treated as a loss and the new rule exempts. Unrestricted, the new strategy bought
2022-11-04 @ 37.97 (+$4,640, trailing stop 12-16), then 2022-12-19 @ 39.40 (−$143 net, +0.3% price —
exempt again), and on **2023-01-04 @ 38.95 rode the recovery to 2023-08-09 for +$28,006** — the trade
the previous 3% rule never took (it stayed restricted on 11-03's loss until a reset on 2023-02-15, by
which time the signal was gone). This single episode is $28,006 + $4,640 = +$32,646 of the +$36,217
total improvement over the previous rule. It hinges on a 0.0076-percentage-point margin: the
2022-11-03 price return is −0.4924%, exempt only because the threshold is −0.5000%.

**October 2023 (a cost of the exemption).** The 2023-09-21 → 10-06 trade lost $503 on a −0.04% price
move; exempt, so the new strategy bought the first signal, 2023-10-20 @ 50.54 (+$15,193). The
previous 3% rule, restricted, waited for 49.27 on 10-26 and earned +$18,490 on the same move: −$3,297
for entering four sessions earlier at a higher price.

**April 2024.** The 2024-04-19 trade (+$14,049) and the 2024-08-01 trade (−$1,806) are identical
across strategies; window differences are mark-to-market timing only.

**March–April 2025.** Identical to the previous 3% rule: the 03-03 loss (−2.57%) armed, the 03-28
re-entry 3% lower lost −$1,753 (−1.24%, still material), and the next 3% level filled 2025-04-16 @ 63.20
for **+$39,335** vs the baseline's 04-11 entry @ 64.74 (+$35,713) — but the baseline also absorbed a
−$7,843 trade on 03-19. The new mechanisms were inert here (no reset fired, no loss was exempt).

**Where G8 (1% cutoff) differs.** The only divergence on this period is the 2024-08-01 → 09-04 loss
(−$1,806, price **−0.78%**): material at 0.5%, exempt at 1%. G8 (like the baseline) re-entered
2024-09-05 @ 67.72 and earned +$4,285 by 2025-02-27; the new strategy was restricted until the
09-12 reset and had no signal again until 2025-03-03. That one trade is the entire $4,285 gap
(G8 $177,168 vs $172,883).

## 4. Where the money comes from

- Versus the previous 3% rule: +$36,217 = +$32,646 (Nov-2022/Jan-2023 chain, material-loss
  exemption) + $7,010 (Dec-2018/Jan-2019 chain, retracement reset) − $3,297 (Oct-2023 earlier entry)
  − ~$140 (minor timing). Three episodes; one of them on a 0.0076pp classification margin.
- Versus the baseline: +$34,538 with a $9,636 smaller drawdown, from avoiding the 2022 cascade
  (−$23,643 → −$165) and the 2018/2020/2025 repeated losses, net of the recoveries the restriction
  skipped (2019 +$6,470 less than the baseline; the baseline's 2024-09 trade).
- Versus the matched-exposure baseline control (fixed $86,522 size, equal dollar-days): control
  $119,700 / $33,158 → the new strategy is **+$53,183 net with $4,471 less drawdown**. The
  improvement is not a deleveraging artifact on this period.
- Dependence: the largest winner (+$48,014, 2020-04-02) is 28% of net profit; excluding it the
  strategy still earns $124,869. The improvement over the previous rule, however, is concentrated in
  the January-2023 trade (77% of it).
