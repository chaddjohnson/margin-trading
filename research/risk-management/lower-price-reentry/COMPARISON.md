# Lower-price re-entry with recovery reset — consolidated comparison

Generated by `tables.py` from `results/summary.json` (run `run.py` first). Rule: after a losing exit,
re-enter only at a close 3% / 5% / 8% below the losing exit price, unless the close first recovers to
within 5% of the losing trade's frozen 90-session entry reference high (recovery reset). Details,
verification and interpretation: `REPORT.md`.
