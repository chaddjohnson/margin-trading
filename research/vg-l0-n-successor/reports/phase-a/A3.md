## 1. Summary of the findings

- **680 lanes** ran: 4 cutoffs × (128 broad + 26 refinement) Track I lanes and 64 Track N lanes at C24 [V]. They
  generated 1,303,708 proposals, 3,302 islands, 2,152 refinement starts and 366,927 moves; 18,265,213 evaluations were
  requested, of which 17,533,469 were computed fresh [V; equals `BUD`].
- **Stop reasons [V].** 495 lanes stopped on "triggers not met", 185 on "extension cap reached"; **0 on the resource
  guard**. The job cap was never close to binding: at the final barrier each job had requested 25.4–43.1% of its cap.
- **Budget-bound share [V].** Track I: 33/154 (21.4%) at C19, 47/154 (30.5%) at C21, 42/154 (27.3%) at C23 and 49/154
  (31.8%) at C24; 171/616 (27.8%) overall. By stage, the broad lanes were 163/512 (31.8%) and the refinement lanes 8/104
  (7.7%). Track N was 14/64 (21.9%).
- **What drove it [V, with the mechanism R].** The structure that best separates bound from unbound lanes is how many
  decline gates are ON. With both gates ON, 110/128 broad lanes (85.9%) were bound; with one gate, 48/256 (18.8%); with
  none, 5/128 (3.9%). The D1 families matter much less: by D1·GDX family the share is 24–38%, by D1·VGT family 23–37%.
  The trigger still firing at the cap was the "new feasible behaviour class" trigger *alone* in 142 of the 185 bound
  lanes: 120 of 163 broad, 8 of 8 refinement and 14 of 14 Track N. The median last-island gains of the bound broad
  lanes were 0.04–0.16% in hypervolume and 0.00% in best centre net. The behaviour class is cell × control pattern ×
  funded components, and the number of possible classes grows as 4 per gated book and 2 per protected book. Under a
  hypothetical metric-only trigger (the same rule without the class term), the recorded trigger logs imply that 5 broad
  lanes, and no refinement or Track N lane, would have been bound [R, §4.5].
- **Islands [V].** Every island used its full proposal allotment (256 broad / 384 refinement and informed; 8 / 12
  generations), except one Track N island that stagnated after 9 generations. Of 3,302 islands, 11 stopped on
  stagnation, 10 of them at their last generation; no other island ended before its proposal allotment.
- **N/original** (`rc1-f1a8b57cb02455c3`, cell GD1.VB0) is the shortlist's profit pick (#1) of lane
  `C24/informed/GD.VB/R2` (job `informed2`, replicate 2). The lane's best centre net reached $388,031.02 during
  base island 3, and the four extension islands that followed added nothing [V]. The lane ran to the cap of 8 islands
  and stopped as budget-bound, on the class trigger alone [V].
- **Replicate noise [V].** The median of |best qualified net R1 − R2| per cell was $5,953 / $13,083 / $16,762 /
  $23,858 at C19 / C21 / C23 / C24, which is 6.2% / 6.3% / 8.9% / 12.3% of the better replicate's net.

## 3. Per-lane execution (item 2)

### 3.1 Stop semantics, exactly as coded

- **Island level** (`search.ts` 600–765). An island runs generations of 32 children each. It stops `budget` when its new
  configurations reach `newPerIsland` (256 broad, 384 refinement/informed). It stops `stagnation` after `patience` = 8
  generations in which neither the island population's hypervolume of (−robust net, p90 DD) against (0, $35,000) rose by
  > 0.1%, nor the lane's best feasible centre net rose by > 0.1% (lines 732–754). The other stops are
  `no new configurations` and `generations` (200). A broad island lasts at most 8 generations (256 / 32), so stagnation
  can only be declared at the 8th generation, after a fully stale island.
- **Trigger** (`search.ts` 1010–1031). The trigger is measured only on the **last** base island and on each extension
  island. It compares the lane's state before and after **that island**, not cumulatively. That state is the
  hypervolume over the whole rung-1 archive, the best feasible robust net, the best feasible centre net, and the count of
  feasible behaviour classes. The trigger *fires* iff the hypervolume, robust or centre-net gain is > 5%, or ≥ 1 new
  feasible behaviour class appeared. A class is `cell | control pattern | funded components` (lines 582–589).
- **Lane level, at barriers** (`stages.ts` 218–256). In each round, every live lane whose last trigger did not fire gets
  `triggers not met`. A lane whose trigger fired while it already holds `islandsMax` islands gets
  `extension cap reached (a trigger still fired: budget-bound, not converged)`. All other live lanes extend by one island,
  unless `requested + Σ round worst case + finishing reserve × lanes > job cap`, in which case they get
  `resource guard`. Then `finish` runs rung 2, rung 3, refinement and the shortlist (`search.ts` 1040–1141).
- **Two consequences [V].**
  - A lane that *reached* the island cap but whose last island did not fire is recorded as **"triggers not met"**: 54
    broad, 3 refinement and 7 Track N lanes (T4). "At cap" and "budget-bound" are therefore different counts.
  - The resource guard could not bind (adaptation AD7). The job cap is Σ per-lane worst case (`budgets.ts` 101–121):
    PLC 41,000 / 110,000 / 114,000, so the cap was 2,624,000 per broad job, 2,860,000 per refinement job and 3,648,000 per
    informed job (`S:*.cap`). The finishing reserve was 8,000 / 30,000 / 30,000 per lane (`PF.finishReserve`).

**Masking, repair and identity [V code].**

- **Pinning (471–522).** In Track I and refinement, the cell's family and gate genes, every inactive D1 or control gene
  and an A book's activation are pinned to index 0 and never varied. In Track N only the families and the
  family-dependent inactive genes are pinned. The gates are free, and the K, r, c of a gate-OFF book stay **dormant**:
  kept in the genome, crossed over, but not decoded.
- **Variation (`search.ts` 522–573).** Categorical uniform crossover (p 0.9) and SBX on grid indices apply to all
  unpinned genes, so dormant genes travel by crossover. Polynomial mutation applies only to *active* ordinals
  (`activeOrdinal`, 378–390). Categorical reset applies at 1/(number of free categoricals). Neighbourhoods and
  refinement moves touch only active inputs (684–806, 849–942); K is fixed in neighbourhoods and moves by ± 1 in
  refinement.
- **Canonical repair (`cfgOf`, 249–271).** A C book's inert hold and buffer are set to their box minimum. A D book's
  extended hold becomes max(hx, hold + 3). An A book's activation is ignored. Growth becomes `effectiveOf(repair(g))`:
  momentum short < long, only active keys kept, validated by Strategy Lab's Growth 1.4.0 validator (s8m `space.ts`
  173–195). `encode` (338–376) writes inactive genes from a dormant parent or seed genome when given, else index 0
  (D1, controls) or growth140's convention (Growth).
- **Identity (392–404).**
  - `cfgId` = RC 2's `riskId2` of the quad (the D1 pair, the Growth·GDX placeholder, the effective Growth·VGT, D1 first)
    and the canonical controls. A legacy configuration (cell GA0.VA0, no controls) keeps Priority 3's 16-hex quadId
    (New L0 = `a2e385640ca61012`). Next-session controls give `rc1-…`, and any entry-day book gives `rc2-…`.
  - Because Growth enters by its effective-parameter identity and the controls are canonical, genomes that differ only
    in inactive or dormant genes map to one configuration and one evaluation key (`evalKeyOf`, 394).
  - Duplicates are rejected at generation time by cfgId: 5,641 broad, 2,330 refinement and 1,728 Track N children
    (§3.2).
- **Distance (441–469).** Normalised L∞ over active numeric inputs; 1 across cells, activation patterns or Growth
  structures.
- **Strata (556–683).** Each broad screen is stratified by activation (2 per protected book) × K (4 per gated book) ×
  NL0's six Growth strata. P ranges from 6 to 384, and the 512 points are allocated greedily for balance.

## 10. Evidence gaps

1. **No island-of-origin field.** No sealed record ties a configuration to the island, generation or rung that produced
   it. That N/original came from base island 3 is inferred from the best-net step (§6). The job archives (`points`,
   `configs`, `fits`) carry no lane, island or generation column.
2. **Rounded trigger strings.** The `triggerLog` reasons round to 0.1%. The exact components were recomputed from
   `islands[].before/after` and agree with every `fired` flag. The rounded strings alone misclassify a few cases at
   exactly "5.0%".
3. **Growth stratum per lane is a proxy.** Broad lanes span all six Growth strata, so "budget-bound by Growth stratum"
   can only be given through a lane-level proxy (the profit pick's stratum, T6, [R]). Per-stratum counts within lanes
   exist (`counts["centres Growth …"]`, `byClass`), but extension decisions are lane-level.
4. **Counterfactual reach.** The metric-only counterfactual (T14) covers island counts and island requests only. The
   rungs, refinement and shortlist after a different stop point were never run, so their outcomes are unknown.
5. **No per-lane wall time or memory.** Lanes share one job (§8).
6. **Rung-1 telemetry only.** Convergence beyond the islands (rungs 2–3, refinement) is recorded only as endpoints and
   paths, not as trajectories.
7. **Track N incumbent rows.** The qualification record holds 3 `incumbent:` rows (New L0, C4, C4R). The handling of IJ1
   as a selectable N member was not traced here; it was not needed for the search reconstruction.
8. **Active Growth input bound.** The 27 stated in `budgets.ts` 94–97 differs from my rule-based count of 28 (§9). This
   was not verified against Strategy Lab's `effectiveParams`.
9. **Pilot calibration records** (`.cache/vg-l0-full-joint/pilot/`) were not read. The pilot figures quoted come from
   PROTOCOL §8.4 only.
