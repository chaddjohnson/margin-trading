- **Written:** 2026-11-24T11:49Z (clock, UTC), branch `research/vg-l0-n-original-successor`, HEAD `5d587bb2`.
- **Scope:** read-only reconstruction from sealed result records, job memory samples, job logs and the executed code. No
  evaluation was run; no market data, job archive (`.cache/…`, `.zst`) or FULL window was opened; no file was changed
  apart from this one.
- **Labels:** **[V]** read directly from a sealed record or log (path and field given) or from the executed code;
  **[V-d]** exact arithmetic on [V] values, cross-checked against an independent [V] total where one exists;
  **[R]** reconstructed or estimated (the method is stated). No recommendations are made.
- **Units used throughout:**
  - **Proposal**: a distinct configuration put to rung 1 (screen proposal, seed design or NSGA-II island child). Rung-1
    **fillers** (Latin-supercube top-ups of a short island population) are counted separately. Refinement moves are
    counted separately.
  - **Requested**: the evaluations a lane asked for (`lanes[].requested`: centres and neighbours, archive hits
    included). In both studies the code does **not** add the independent screen to `requested` (3B
    `src/vg-d1-growth-expanded/search.ts` `screen()`; 5B `src/vg-lifecycle-matched-optimization/search.ts` `screen()`), so
    "requested (incl. screen)" adds the screen tally.
  - **Unique fresh**: simulations computed by the job (`compute.fresh`; one per archive write). **Archive hits**:
    `compute.archiveHits`. **Within-batch duplicates**: requested − fresh − hits (the lab evaluates a key once per batch
    and counts neither a fresh run nor a hit for the repeat: `lab.ts` `evalMany`, both studies).
  - **Generation**: one NSGA-II generation of `population` children.

## Comparability to the three-book V scope

- **Same structure.** In both studies "V" is D1·GDX + D1·VGT + Growth·VGT with Growth·GDX disabled, carrying Priority
  3's placeholder vector and never funded. Both use the same genome (3 inclusion + 17 categorical + 116 ordinal genes),
  boxes, grids and cfgId [V `config.ts` `growthOn`/`optionsOfInc` in both; 5B's header states that it is 3B's
  unchanged]. Both studies evaluate under L0 (5B: the L0 arm only).
- **3B independent.** Only the two V cells per cutoff (entry pinned: VGT rise or breakout) search V:
  - 143,360 of 557,056 independent proposals (25.7 %);
  - 1,278,470 of 4,888,001 independent requested evaluations (26.2 %);
  - ≈ 1.27 M of the 4.87 M independent fresh [R];
  - 38 of 144 islands; 3,464 of 22,604 refinement moves.

  The G and B cells evaluate other inclusions. A B configuration became V by refinement once (C24, disclosure A2).
  Per cutoff the V share is 22.8–27.8 % of requested (§3B.5).
- **3B informed.** Inclusion was free. V took 60.6 % (fixed) and 66.7 % (joint) of rung-1 centres, but evaluations by
  inclusion are not recorded. In informed-fixed, VG #2's D1 vectors were pinned, and (disclosure A1) its search-time
  neighbourhoods measured Growth-only robustness, so that lane does not search a three-book space's D1 genes.
- **5B L0 independent.** The V cell (two replicates per cutoff; entry methods as screen strata, not pinned) took 26.0–
  33.0 % of L0 requested per cutoff: 120,970 of 415,049 requested including screens, 12,032 of 45,312 executed
  proposals, 31 of 113 islands and 1,930 of 8,089 refinement moves.
- **5B L0 informed.** Inclusion was free. V took ≈ 73 % of rung-1 centres, and the lane started from 4 V seeds
  (C4, C4R, C85, IJ1).
- **Effective search on V only (both informed lanes excluded)**: 3B ≈ 1.28 M requested over 4 cutoffs (≈ 0.30–0.33 M
  per cutoff); 5B L0 ≈ 0.12 M over 4 cutoffs (≈ 0.03 M per cutoff) [V-d requested; fresh slightly lower].

## Evidence gaps

1. **Fresh simulations are tallied per job and stage tag only**, never per lane, cell or (5B) arm. Every per-lane,
   per-cell or per-arm figure is "requested"; per-arm and per-cell fresh values are estimates [R].
2. **Compute time per lane and per arm is not recorded.** 5B's L0 share of wall and worker time cannot be separated
   (L1/L2 evaluations cost more).
3. **Proposal provenance is not recorded** (whether a member is a design or an island child, which island, which
   generation). The origin of IJ1's refinement start `adee768dd095ae01` is unknown, as is New L0's island
   (NL0 §5 already says so for New L0).
4. **3B, per lane:** the requested split between promoted-screen rung 1 and refinement cannot be separated exactly,
   because filler neighbour counts are not recorded. The job-level tag split is exact.
5. **Island improvement at the budget stop** (§3B.6) is an approximation from rounded trajectories [R]. The records
   hold no "would have stagnated" flag.
6. **5B C24 qualification:** unique fresh per arm is not recorded (3,909 duplicated member evaluations across the job).
7. **The discarded 5B A1 run** (11,761 evaluations) and the engineering, pilot and bench evaluations of both studies are
   not in the sealed search records. The figures come from the reports [R]; job archives were not opened.
8. **Logs are git-ignored local files.** Only worker counts and job starts were taken from them; the same facts are in
   the committed memory samples.
