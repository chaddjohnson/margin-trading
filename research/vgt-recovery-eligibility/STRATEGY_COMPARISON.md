What the mechanisms change, trade by trade (full lists in `finalists.md`):

- **D1** adds exactly six continuation entries to Set 2's fourteen: 2019-08-05 → 2020-02-24 (+$17,713, after
  the May 2019 exit), 2020-02-28 → 2020-03-06 (+$2,398, stopped out profitably one week before the crash),
  2020-10-28 → 2021-02-23 (+$13,147, after the September 2020 exit), 2021-03-05 → 2021-05-11 (+$3,419),
  2022-04-26 → 2022-05-06 (−$1,077) and 2022-09-16 → 2022-09-26 (−$3,187). None of Set 2's own trades
  changes. The two losses are the whole 2022 cost; the April 2025 exit opened a window that the April 11
  deep arming closed.
- **D3** (7%) arms on shallower pullbacks and therefore at different times: 2019-06-03 → 2019-08-05
  (+$5,633, two months before D1's August entry and worth $12k less), 2020-09-21 → 2020-10-26 (+$4,213),
  2022-04-22 → 2022-05-02 (−$2,437), 2022-09-06 → 2022-09-15 (−$187), 2023-03-13 → 2023-08-08 (+$19,542) and
  2023-09-21 → 2023-09-29 (−$451): six continuation trades for +$26,313, with the 2023 participation paid
  for by weaker 2019 and 2020 re-entries.
- **B1** adds one trade (2023-01-18 → 2023-08-08) and nothing else.
- **BD1** is D1 plus B1 plus one continuation loss after the August 2023 exit (−$1,175); BD2's jointly tuned
  numbers give the same trades within a few dollars.
- **A1** adds 2022-12-28 → 2023-03-13 (+$8,177): the entry Trades2 made, closed by Set 2's tighter stop.

| BD1 | $201,986 / $19,861 | $202,704 / $19,856 | **$192,423** / $19,183 | $173,738 / $19,479 |

The idealized stop fill matters by under $1,000 on every finalist. Interest moves Set 2 and D1 by about
±$6–9k per three points without changing a ranking, but **B1's 2023 trade disappears at 8%**: with less
accrued interest the 7.3% trailing stop is active (above breakeven) during the 2023-03-13 pullback and closes
the January 18 entry for a few hundred dollars instead of riding to August (B1 at 8% is $280 above Set 2 at
8%). At 11% the stop was still below breakeven on that day and the trade survived. BD1 inherits the same
sensitivity. The trough mechanism's single trade therefore depends on the entry threshold (T 16–18%), the stop
width (7.3% versus Trades2's 7.8%) *and* the interest-rate assumption all lining up.
