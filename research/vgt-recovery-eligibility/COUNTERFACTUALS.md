### What the first run showed

- **Every episode mechanism (A, B, E) was inert at the predeclared 50% reset.** The October 2022 episode
  (anchor 391.60, trough 291.62) ended on 2022-11-30, when the close of 347.94 recovered more than half of
  the decline *while trade 11 was still open*; after the December 15 exit there was no episode left to act
  on. A 38% reset is the same. This is why the next block was added.
- **C is "lower X" in disguise.** With Y2 = Y the dual rows equal the global-X controls to the dollar; with
  Y2 = 3% they lose less in 2022 but still $25–43k below Set 2 at $29–36k drawdowns.
- **D at 8% is the only predeclared setting that beats Set 2 on both axes** (+$14.8k to +$20.7k at $19,806),
  from the 2019 and 2020 continuation trades, with −$7k in 2022 and nothing in 2023.

### Reading

1. **A with the latest anchor can never fire in 2023** (20.76% below 391.60); with the highest anchor it is
   the 365-session lookback under another name, and loses $14,050 with a $35,099 drawdown for the same
   reason (LOOKBACK_CONTROLS.md section 2). Bounding the retention (expiry 60/120) removes the 2023 trade
   before it removes the 2022 losses.
2. **E is strictly worse than A**: the shallower the latch, the more bear-market re-entries ($46,104
   drawdown at U ≤ 10%).
3. **B at 5–10% enters on the first flat session (2022-12-16 at 330.21) and loses $4,047** to the 8-day
   hold; the one-shot rule then blocks the window because December 28 did not undercut the October trough.
   The settings that work (17%, found by the sweeps) enter on 2023-01-18.
4. **D is the only mechanism that improves Set 2 without reference to 2023**, and its 2022 cost (two
   continuation losses of −$1,077 and −$3,187 after the April and August 2022 exits) is bounded by the
   one-per-window and profitable-exit-only rules.

The sweeps and the optimization in OPTIMIZATION.md start from these settings.
