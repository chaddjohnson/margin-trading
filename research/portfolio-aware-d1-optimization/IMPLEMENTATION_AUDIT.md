## 2. Known defect in a record

The protocol freeze record (`results/protocol_freeze.json`, 2026-10-24T14:54:55Z) lists its first modified file as
`onfig/phenotypes/COMPATIBILITY.md`: the git-status parser trimmed the line's leading space. The freeze is
write-once, so the record stays as written; the parser was fixed before any later record (`gitState` in `freeze.ts`),
and the protocol hash it records is unaffected.

## 3. Changes after the selection freeze (2026-10-24T15:37:44Z)

`FROZEN_SELECTION.json` records the sha256 of every study file at the freeze. Three differ now, none in the evaluation
logic: `cli.ts` gained the `post-hoc` and `run` commands; `evaluation.ts` gained `runPostHoc` (non-decisional
diagnostic) and `runFrozenConfiguration` (the post-study `run` command) appended after `runEvaluation`, which is
unchanged; `report.ts` was re-formatted by prettier (and its evaluation tables were written after the evaluation). The
evaluation and its first confirmation ran on the frozen code; a second confirmation with the current code (§5)
reproduced the same record digest. Also added after the evaluation, as the protocol allows for an accepted
configuration: `config/phenotypes/D1/GDX.json` (new file), and the `.gitignore` lines for the regenerable trial logs.

The new tests (`src/portfolio-aware-d1/tests/engine.test.ts`):

1. the N-book engine equals the frozen engine on a four-book synthetic universe, fixed and portfolio, at 0/5/10 bps;
2. it funds a candidate book under the same rules (refusal while held; release at the exit; GDX wins a same-minute tie
   alphabetically);
3. the replay equals the N-book engine field for field on five-book, substitution and standalone pools at 0/2/5/10 bps;
4. causality: altering a funded trade's exit price, or moving its exit two sessions later, changes no earlier decision;
5. the trace cache key changes with each of eleven behaviour-relevant inputs;
6. a candidate lot held across a synthetic 1:4 reverse split equals the unsplit economy on the N-book engine and the
   replay (the unadjusted bytes would manufacture more than $100,000);
7. optimization access refuses holdout, final and all before reading a bar, and lets training through;
8. the post-freeze loader refuses a missing, tampered or rewritten selection manifest.

## 5. Parity and determinism

- **Controls (§9):** all reproduced to the cent; 40 frozen CSVs byte-identical; canonical path = legacy path; full
  history = current artifacts (DATA_AND_BASELINE_AUDIT.md §4).
- **E1–E3:** 1,208 comparisons, 0 differences (CACHE_VS_FULL_ENGINE.md §4).
- **E4:** the six champions on the full engine (addition and standalone, four costs: 48 runs) — exact; the six Stage C
  configurations (fixed, four costs: 24) — exact; the six frozen configurations on `all` (fixed, four costs: 24) — exact.
- **Δ decomposition:** own lots + gained − displaced = Δ to 1e-6 on all 14,540 Stage A evaluations, and to 1e-9 on the
  evaluation bridges.
- **Determinism:** the evaluation re-run (`evaluate --confirm`) reproduced the record digest `91d39a77…` exactly, twice
  (frozen code, and current code). Stage A–C are seeded (Sobol scramble and NSGA-II seeds in `FROZEN_SELECTION.json`),
  single-threaded and order-deterministic.
- **The registered phenotype:** `npm run app -- allIn-trailingBuy-postExitContinuation GDX --phenotype portfolioAware`
  (the strategy file through the phenotype injection) gives $144,885.01 / $33,555.21 / 18 trades on training, equal to
  the study's replay ($144,885.0134 / $33,555.2131 / 18); on `all` its holdout ($24,707.09) and final ($31,724) equal
  the 0 bps post-training deltas of the evaluation.
