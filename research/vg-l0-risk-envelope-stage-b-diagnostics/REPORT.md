# RES-BD 1.0.0 — Stage B final diagnostics: the search-archive census and the profit-giveback decomposition

This report has written analysis and Markdown tables only. It is a **post hoc, report-only supplement** to the sealed
Stage B study (RES-B 1.0.0). Stage B's preregistered label, **P3 non-inferior and superior** on paired training-period
profit at C21 and C24, and its recommendation, **do not adopt P3; keep P0; return to staged trailing stops**, are quoted
unchanged and are not replaced.

**What was performed.**
- No genetic optimization or candidate search.
- No GPU evaluation.
- No new evaluation in the census.
- Bounded CPU reference replays of 199 fixed, sealed subjects. They match the official GPU records to the cent and bit
  for bit.

Every figure comes from the records in `results/`, generated by `tools/vg-l0-risk-envelope-stage-b-diagnostics/`:
- `results/analysis/TABLES.md`;
- `results/census/`, `results/qualification/`, `results/giveback/`;
- `results/verification/`.

**Evidence levels.** Four kinds of evidence are kept apart and labelled as such:
- **(a)** the search-population differences (the census);
- **(b)** qualified-finalist outcomes (Stage B's sealed qualification, re-verified);
- **(c)** the trade- and episode-level risk decomposition of the selections (the giveback replays);
- **(d)** the later interval behaviour (Stage B's sealed intervals, quoted only).

The history is VGT / GDX 2016–2026, examined by many studies before. It is **never a pristine out-of-sample test**.

## 0. Executive answers

1. **Does P3 mostly allow greater profit giveback, or greater capital erosion?**
   - **At the selected centres: mostly giveback.** P3's lane representatives have +$1.9k / +$1.6k / +$3.0k more
     conventional marked DD (C19 / C21 / C24, 32 paired lanes each). Of that increase:
     - **92 % / 78 % / 72 %** is unbanked giveback of open profit: ΔU +$1,717 / +$1,239 / +$2,125;
     - the rest is banked-profit erosion: ΔE +$151 / +$350 / +$1,232, every 80 % interval including 0;
     - starting-capital loss is **zero** in every P3 representative.
   - **Not purely giveback.** The selections P0 actually rejects were rejected on neighbourhood DD. At C21 their extra
     centre DD is banked erosion (+$1.8k, against −$0.4k giveback). Also at C21, the neighbourhood p90 banked-capital
     DD (+$2.3k) and the p90 trade principal loss (+$3.2k) rose with the p90 DD (+$4.6k); both 80 % intervals exclude
     zero. (GIVEBACK_REPORT §4–§5.)
2. **How many searched configurations pass only P3, only P0, both or neither?** Over the 72,595 / 82,868 / 88,377
   profitable, funded centres visited:
   - P3 only **2,256 / 5,918 / 7,534**;
   - P0 only **284 / 514 / 375**;
   - both 66,314 / 62,410 / 47,285;
   - neither 3,741 / 14,026 / 33,183.
   - Finalists: P3 only 6 / 30 / 36 and P0 only **0** (Stage B's sealed verdicts, reproduced).
   - **Binding limits.** P0 binds only on marked DD. P3 binds on DD > $40k and BD > $30k, often together.
   - P3's PL and BD gates exclude about 27 % of the ($35k, $40k] band centres, but only at the screen or early rungs.
     They therefore almost never constrain finalists. (CENSUS_REPORT §3–§4.)
3. **Are P3's higher conventional and neighbourhood DDs largely winning-trade giveback?**
   - **Conventional (centre) DD: largely yes,** and banked-capital DD and trade principal loss are unchanged at the
     centre.
   - **Neighbourhood DD: only partly.**
     - At C19 and C24, the increase in the confirmation p90 DD is matched by the provable giveback bound.
     - At C21 there is evidence of **materially worse downside exposure** (higher p90 BD and p90 PL).
     - P3's breaching draws (> $35k) are 2–4 × as frequent. Only 10–32 % of them are provably giveback-dominated.
   - The sealed retrospective intervals had larger DD under P3 at C21 and C24 (Stage B, quoted).
4. **Is there a reason to revisit a profit-component-aware selector, or to close the risk-envelope work?**
   - **Close the envelope work and proceed to staged trailing stops.**
   - The diagnostics show that the conventional drawdown of **both** arms' selections is mostly giveback of winning
     open positions: about 90 % at C19 / C21 and about 60 % at C24. That giveback happens before Growth's trend exit or
     a trailing stop fires, often through a gapped fill. The loss below principal comes mostly from D1 lots held to
     maximum hold.
   - That is a direct argument for **exit-side** research, which is Priority 3–4, rather than for loosening the
     qualification ceiling.
   - A profit-component-aware selector remains a **hypothesis** for a separately authorized future test on new data,
     not a validated method. Nothing here shows that admitting giveback-type drawdowns improves later outcomes, and
     Stage B's intervals point the other way.
5. **What remains unavailable without new data or a separate authorized study?**
   - The exact D3 of every neighbourhood draw and of the census population; here there is only a lower bound.
   - Any out-of-sample evidence beyond 2026-10-30 or on other instruments.
   - Whether a giveback-aware criterion would select differently and better, which needs a preauthorized search.
   - Margin-account, execution-gap and tax effects beyond the recorded fills.

**Does this change the interpretation of P3?** It **refines** it without overturning it.
- **The finding.** P3's centre-level extra drawdown is mostly profit giveback rather than capital erosion; that is new
  and somewhat more benign than "more drawdown". P3 also brings no principal or banked-capital improvement, confirmed
  over the whole searched population.
- **The cautions.** Its neighbourhood-level extra risk is not shown to be benign, and at C21 it is not. Its
  training-profit advantage at the pooled level is a search-path effect, not eligibility. Its next intervals were
  unfavourable.
- **The verdict.** Stage B's verdict and the recommendation not to adopt P3 stand. Nothing here strengthens the case for
  P3. It **does** strengthen a narrower hypothesis: much of this strategy family's conventional drawdown is open-profit
  giveback, the target of exit design.

## 2. Diagnostic A in brief (CENSUS_REPORT.md)

- **Coverage.** The full archives: 1.12 M / 1.14 M / 0.95 M evaluated keys. Every centre is classified, not only
  finalists. The census reproduces Stage B's sealed screen plausibility and breach counts exactly.
- **Eligibility.**
  - P3 admits the ($35k, $40k] marked-DD band, about 73 % of it; its PL and BD gates exclude the other 27 %.
  - A small P0-only set exists at the **centre** level: 0.4–0.6 % of scope, excluded by P3's BD or PL gates, never past
    refinement and less profitable. That is why Stage B saw CTRL-only = 0 among finalists.
- **Search path.**
  - The EXP-30 lanes spent more of their post-screen search in the band: 1.4–1.8 × CTRL's P3-only centres.
  - The single best centre at every cutoff is plausible and qualified under **both** policies. At C21 and C24 it was
    found by an EXP-30 lane but is P0-qualified, so the pooled-level gain is a search-path difference.
- **Rung fits.** P3's extra feasible fits at every rung are exactly those with neighbourhood p90 DD in ($35k, $40k].
- **Bound at the centre.** The giveback bound shows the P3-only admissions are **not** more giveback-like than the
  shared population: mean provable share 45 / 41 / 36 %, against 62 / 45 / 37 %.

**Winners versus losers.**
- Inside the maximum-DD windows, nearly all giveback is by lots that **closed profitable**: Growth trend exits,
  trailing stops and gapped trailing-stop fills. Much of it at C19 and C21 is in lots still open at the cutoff.
- Deepening below principal is concentrated in D1 lots **held to maximum hold** and closed at a loss. P3 does not
  increase it.

## 5. Limitations

- **One market history, retrospective.** The cutoffs are nested, so results are reported per cutoff and never pooled.
  Two cells; path-dependent lanes.
- **Coverage of the exact partition.** It covers the centre's single maximum-DD window, for 199 subjects only. The
  neighbourhood and the population have a lower bound only; the bound can prove giveback but never prove erosion.
- **These are diagnostics of frozen selections.** They cannot show that a different selector would do better.
- **Replay counts.** The CPU replays include one stopped and repeated C19 run (D3), so 798 engine runs in all against a
  planned 630 (O3). The census and qualification replays evaluated nothing.

## 6. Recommendation (DECISION_REGISTER.md; nothing adopted)

- Keep P0 as the standard.
- Close the risk-envelope investigation.
- Proceed to **Priority 3: the D1 / Growth trailing-stop audit**, then Priority 4, with these diagnostics as its
  motivating evidence: giveback before trend exits and trailing stops (including gaps), and D1 maximum-hold losses.
- Keep the Stage B job archives until the user's review. They could be compressed after it, using the existing
  `.sqlite.zst` practice with hashes and a restore test; they are not deleted here.
