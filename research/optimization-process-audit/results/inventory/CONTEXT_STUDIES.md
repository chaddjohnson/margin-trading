**Mechanisms the context studies themselves report** (their interpretations, marked as such there):

- Priority 2: fold primaries "fit on very few trades" (one D1 lot and one Growth lot in four years at C19); "their
  neighbourhoods looked stable because those few winning trades survive small perturbations"; lesson "count trades and
  episodes before reading neighbourhood stability".
- growth140-vgt-stable: rank persistence between folds Spearman −0.15 to −0.50; C19 members carried December 2018
  positions into the 2020 crash; C21 members carried March–April 2020 positions into the 2022 decline; "the training
  neighbourhoods described 2016–2019 and 2016–2021, not the next regime"; screens produced 2,312–2,908 distinct ledgers
  per 4,096 Rise configurations; none of #4's 1,024 neighbours reproduced its ledger.
- Priority 3: largest episodes 38.5–39.9% of the primaries' net; confirmation draws 339 of 384 clipped for the primary;
  one Growth-pair member had only 125 distinct ledgers in 384 draws; the validation loss came from a long Growth·VGT
  hold occupying the slot.
- Priority 5A: lifecycle changes did little (median paired Δ within ±$2,500); the cost of GDX to VGT is the refusal at
  VGT's trend entry; L1/L2 freed Growth entries displaced better D1 trades (IP −$83,057.12).
- Priority 3C: no independent lane (departure D1); the 2019 gold rally is 80–120% of the R partners' increments; the
  March 2020 rebound carries the G primary; near-dominance labels flip with the instrument.

**Historical proposals recorded there (not instructions here):** benchmark the plain combination; prefer few
structured degrees of freedom; weight chronological evidence; count trades and episodes before reading neighbourhood
stability; Priority 3C's roadmap entry approving this process investigation (investigation only).
