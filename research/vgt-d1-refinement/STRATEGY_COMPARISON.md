# Strategy comparison

All candidates on the common full run (arming from 2017-06-15, 2016-01-04 → 2025-10-13 minute data, $100,000 per
entry, 11% interest, stop fills), generated by `npm run d1-refinement -- finalists` (`results/finalists/finalists.md`
holds every candidate's trade list, episode attribution, regime table, sensitivities, neighbourhood and validation
nets; `finalists.csv` / `finalists.json` the same machine-readably). "Evidence": reference = unchanged comparator;
predeclared = fixed before any result; in-sample = chosen after inspecting the full run. No candidate has
out-of-sample evidence: the data have all been inspected.

## 4. Interest and fills

Next-open stop fills move every candidate by under $1,000 (−$2,000 to +$1,000 for the no-dip ones). Three points of
interest move the D1 family by ±$9–10k with an unchanged trade list for D1, A1-p86, A2-lowDd and all five stop
policies at D1's parameters; the trade list changes with the rate for D3, A1-p92w69, A1-p89w169, A2-maxNet (a
hold-day or buffered exit flips) and for every C candidate (C1-maxNet: $170k at 8%, $120k at 14%; C2-maxNet $195k /
$131k). The no-dip strategies' results are the most interest-dependent in the comparison because they are invested
most of the time.
