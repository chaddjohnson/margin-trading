## 6. Limitations

- **Distribution convention.** Fixed mode is price-only for every lot; the portfolio books VGT/VOOG core and VGT-lot
  distributions only. High-yield books (EWZ, XLE, XLU, TLT, KRE) are understated in fitness. No candidate reached a
  post-training evaluation, so no omission bound is reported for one; the convention is the frozen one.
- **VOOG execution.** Fills at actual sparse bars are honest to the data but optimistic for real size (median bar 311
  shares in training).
- **Search pressure.** The drawdown objective treats a zero-trade member as free, so 10.5% of discovery evaluations
  funded nothing; the books objective concentrated the islands on 1–5-book subsets. Neither changed the outcome: the
  binding gate (T6) is failed by every configuration found and by every benchmark.
- **Inherited fit.** The leaders reuse control vectors that earlier studies optimized on the same training years.
- **Gate calibration.** The 75% joint-neighbourhood threshold was set before any neighbourhood was measured; it turned out
  to be stricter than any D1 pool can meet. It is reported as such, not changed (no rescue).
