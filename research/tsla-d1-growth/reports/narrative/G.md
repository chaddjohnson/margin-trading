# Standalone Growth·TSLA (arm G)

**What it is.** Growth 1.4.0 alone on TSLA (D1 off) in the one $100,000 slot: the complete genome on growth140's boxes, 6
protected cells — exit protection {hold off; hold on, buffer off; hold on, buffer on} × initial ATR stop OFF/ON — with
`G.h0.s0` the **unrestricted** (open-ended) Growth kept as its own report-only population (G-U) beside the protected
cells (G-P). 8 base replicates per cell, NL0's lanes, then 4 informed refinement lanes in each cell.

**Development.** C19 `G.h1.s1/R102#1` $150,160 / DD $24,038 (7 lots); C21 `G.h1.s1/R101#1` $303,551 / $32,292 (10 lots,
all winners); C23 `G.h1.s1/R103#1` $302,508 / $30,186 (20 lots). The selected cell was the same each time (hold on,
buffer off, initial stop on), with different vectors.

**Sparse winners (brief Stage B: not favoured on them).** The development nets rest on very few lots and one year:
- C19: 46.5 % of the training net was one lot open at the cutoff (entered 2019-09-27, marked $69,772 on 2019-12-31).
- C21: 69 % came from lots entered in 2020 ($208,541 of $303,551).
- C23: 56 % came from 2020 lots ($168,089 of $302,508).

Q4 (confirmation p10 net > 0) was the gate Growth members failed most often (28–52 of 144–198 broad members per
cutoff). The neighbourhood of a sparse-trade vector often loses the one decisive trade.

**Profitable exits with large drawdowns.** The shared $32,291.84 development drawdown of C21 (G, G-U, C, S, S-U) is one
Growth lot marked at $190,508 at 09:45 ET on 2020-07-13 that fell to the session's 15:55 ET low. The lot still closed
with a gain. This is a give-back of unrealized gain, not a loss of principal: the drawdown cap binds on a single intraday
reversal of a lot that was up about 90 %.

**Chronology (the binding test).** Chained, G's procedure earned +$30,546 (2020–2021), $0 (2022–2023: the C21 primary
never entered) and +$12,401 (2024): **$42,947 with a $36,079 drawdown — unsupported, by $1,079**. The drawdown runs from
an early-January 2020 mark of $48,930, when the lot carried from 2019 was still open (it exited on 2020-01-16 at +$30,546
for the slice), to the 2024 low. That low is −$17,695 below the 2023 close, after an initial stop of −$11,659
(2024-10-28 → 11-04), a −$5,051 trailing-stop exit and a lot entered on 2024-12-26 that was marked −$13,420 at year end. During TSLA's tenfold rise of 2020–2021 the
C19 primary opened no new lot.

**Unrestricted Growth (G-U, report-only).** Its chain earned $300,320, by holding TSLA through 2020–2021, with a
**$111,944** drawdown. That is more than the whole $100,000 slot, because a winning lot's marked value rose several-fold
and then fell back. It is the clearest case of structurally unbounded exposure in the study.

**Descriptive role chains.** G's *balanced* ($50,723 / $30,339) and *lower-drawdown* ($70,436 / $17,275) role chains pass
the numeric chronological test, the only role chains of any arm that do. They are not the frozen selector's choice, so
they are reported and not promoted. Adopting a role rule now, after seeing the folds, would be a change of selection
rule.
