- **Calendar**: through 2025-10-13 the five files hold the same 2,459 dates with the same scheduled open and close
  (0 date and 0 schedule mismatches); XBI, XRT and KRE add 2025-10-14/15/16, which no portfolio or multi-symbol run
  uses. The latest common usable session is **2025-10-13**.
- **Synchrony**: the files do not trade in the same minutes. VOOG trades in 40% of scheduled minutes, VGT in 83%; of
  VGT's minutes only 42.1% also have a VOOG bar. The engine never treats bars of different files as synchronous: each
  security is marked at its own latest completed minute (stale-mark ages are logged at every decision and closest
  approach), each order fills at its own security's next trade.
- **Raw versus adjusted**: every price is raw. No split-sized overnight gap in any file (largest gaps: KRE −14.0% on

## 3. Distributions

- **VGT and VOOG**: the 78-row ledger of the earlier portfolio studies, re-derived by that program's audit from the
  adjusted daily files: VGT 35 of 39 ledger ex-dates matched by a raw/adjusted step (the four 2016 rows predate the
  adjusted history; 2020-03-09/10 is a one-day date offset), VOOG 35 of 39 (three 2016 rows, 2024-03-22). Amounts
  differ by more than 1% on 7 VGT and 9 VOOG rows (the adjusted prices' rounding). Pay dates are estimated (ex-date + 3
  sessions before 2024-05-28, + 2 after). Used exactly as the earlier study used it, so B reproduces.
- **XBI, XRT, KRE**: the repository holds no per-share distribution history, and the history found online
  (third-party aggregators) is partial and not a primary document. **Funded sector lots are price-only
  (non-total-return)**: an ex-date drop is a price move, no cash is credited. This slightly understates every C run
  (sensitivity: `results/stress/sector_yield_bound.csv`, an analytic bound labelled as such).
- The fixed pool is price-only for every symbol, as the D1 research.

## 6. Ledger integrity

`npm run multi-etf-allocator -- audit-ledger` recomputes every principal run from its exported CSVs only
(`results/audit/ledger_audit.json`): share inventories replayed from the signed changes of every event, the identity
assets − liabilities = equity at every event and close, no cash moved and principal = purchase at every entry,
interest = principal × 11% × days / 365 and net = proceeds − principal − interest for every lot, Σ lot nets = realized,
one entitlement per ex-date/source/lot, the pool cap at every entry, every requirement schedule recomputed from the
per-security values. 23 runs (A, B, F-VGT, and both modes × all-primary/all-lowDd × five policies): ≈ 23,600–28,900
checks per portfolio run, **0 failures**. The engine additionally asserts the identity to $0.01, non-negative cash and
inventories, and the pool cap at every reservation and fill, and would stop the run on any breach.
