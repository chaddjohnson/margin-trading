strategy mark (realized + unrealized, interest accrued) and the 20% portfolio's equity, from the last close of one

research phenotype**; the regimes are the source study's G1–G13, the last clipped to the cutoff.

## Findings

- **2020 dominates almost every phenotype**, as D1's design predicts: GDX made $102,406 of its $185,534 in 2020
  ($95,112 in the COVID crash and first rebound, G7); VGT primary $75,296 of $135,359; SLV $75,737 of $103,793; XME
  $93,446 of $216,940. These are large captures of one shared market event, counted once in the independence tests.
- **Breadth differs.** EWW earned in five calendar years (2018 $12,605, 2019 $21,737, 2020 $37,236, 2022 $22,822,
  2023 $9,877), with no losing year; GDX in all seven; VGT primary in five; XME in three (2020–2022) and nothing in
  2017–2019 or 2023; FXI in three (2020, 2022, 2023).
- **2022 and late 2022** are the second shared source: GDX $37,521 in 2022, FXI $56,137 (its October 2022 rebound alone
  $40,292), XME $64,117, VGT primary $22,145, EWW $22,822.
- Years without a single lot (VGT primary 2017 and 2023; XME 2017–2019 and 2023) are normal for a strategy that waits
  for deep declines; they are why a one-slot standalone ties up no margin for long periods.
